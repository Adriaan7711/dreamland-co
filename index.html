How it works

The colour code, explained

Every Dreamland pillow carries an embroidered label. The colour tells you the firmness, and the firmer the pillow, the more filling inside.

  1. Blue Soft 600g
  2. Green Medium 700g
  3. Orange Firm 800g
  4. Red Extra Firm 900g
Softer Firmer

Fill weight is Unicurl Deluxe fibre per pillow. More fill = a fuller, firmer pillow.
