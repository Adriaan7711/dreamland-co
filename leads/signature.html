Adding it to your email

Do this on a laptop or desktop computer. The signature editors are on the web versions of Gmail and Zoho Mail.

Gmail / Google Workspace
  1. On this page, click Copy signature.
  2. Open mail.google.com and sign in with the Dreamland address.
  3. Click the gear icon (top right), then See all settings.
  4. On the General tab, scroll down to Signature and click Create new. Name it Dreamland.
  5. Click in the big signature box and paste (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac). The logo and colours should appear.
  6. Under Signature defaults, choose Dreamland for both For new emails use and On reply/forward use.
  7. Scroll to the very bottom and click Save Changes.
  8. Send yourself a test email to check it.
Zoho Mail
  1. On this page, click Copy signature.
  2. Open mail.zoho.com and sign in with the Dreamland address.
  3. Click the gear icon (Settings), then Signature. If you don't see it, type “signature” in the settings search box.
  4. Click Add new signature (or the +). Name it Dreamland.
  5. Click in the editor and paste (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V).
    If the formatting looks wrong, delete it, click Copy HTML code here instead, open the editor's Insert HTML or source-code option (the </> icon), and paste it there.
  6. Next to the signature, link it to your Dreamland From address, so it's added to new emails and replies.
  7. Click Save and send yourself a test email.
On your phone

The Gmail and Zoho Mail phone apps usually use their own, simpler signature rather than the one above. To keep emails sent from your phone looking right:

  • Gmail app: tap the menu (☰), then Settings, your Dreamland account, and Mobile signature. Paste the plain-text version from this page.
  • Zoho Mail app: tap the menu, then Settings, your account, and Signature. If your signature from the web isn't there, paste the plain-text version.
  • The Email button in the lead tracker opens whichever email app is set as the default on the phone.
  • For an important first email with the full branded signature, send it from a laptop.
Logo not showing?
  • The logo loads from your website, so it only appears once the site is published. Open this page from your live site, not from a file on your computer, before you copy.
  • Some email programs hide images until the reader clicks Show images. The rest of the signature is ordinary text, so it still reads correctly.
  • When you connect your own domain later, open this page on the new address and copy the signature again. The logo link will then use your domain.
