Choose your list as an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file. The first row must hold the column names.

Match each lead field to a column in your file. We've guessed where we could, so please check them. The example comes from the first row.

Each row is matched to a lead by business name first, then email, then phone number, so map at least one of those. The Notes column is only used for rows you choose to add as new leads.
