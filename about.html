About us

The story behind the colour code

A small South African pillow brand with one simple idea: you should know how a pillow will feel before you take it home.

A better night's sleep, made simple

Dreamland Co. was started in 2021 by Gousia Ferreira and James Prinsloo, who felt that buying a pillow had become far too much guesswork.

In the shop every white pillow looks the same, and you only find out whether it's too soft or too firm once you're home. So we gave every Dreamland pillow an embroidered colour code: blue for soft, green for medium, orange for firm and red for extra firm. More filling means a firmer pillow. Simple.

Our pillows are made locally in the Mpumalanga Highveld. Each one is filled with Unicurl Deluxe fibre, closed in a supreme twill casing and checked by hand before it's packed.

Today you'll find Dreamland at pharmacies and shops in Newcastle, Wakkerstroom, Standerton, Ermelo and Volksrust, and we still look after every direct order personally, by WhatsApp or phone.
